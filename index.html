Where Hallucinations Live

A Cross-Architecture Circuit in VQ-Tokenized Vision-Language Models

Shamanthak Hegde*, Xiangrui Liu, Maitreya Patel†, Yezhou Yang
▶Arizona State University ▶Adobe

*Corresponding author: shamanthak@asu.edu

†Partial work done while at Arizona State University

arXiv Code BibTeX

Abstract

Unified vision-language models (VLMs) that tokenize images through a vector-quantized (VQ) codebook routinely hallucinate objects on grounded yes/no benchmarks, yet existing decoding-time fixes treat this as generic miscalibration without an architectural account. Using activation patching across twenty-five models spanning eight LLM families, we identify an early-layer (L0) attention routing circuit shared across VQ-tokenized VLMs and propose a three-gate diagnostic that distinguishes the models carrying it. The diagnostic isolates ten positive models (five natural unified-VQ VLMs across three LLM families and five induced variants) and rejects the remaining fifteen. A single-variable architectural swap (LLaVA-1.6 CLIP+MLP to VQ+Linear) installs the circuit, while a matched-compute MLP control does not, isolating vector quantization as the source of the pathological signal; the routing pathway that carries it is one that the backbone already provides. Against tuned baselines, DoLA wins on binary calibration, but only L0 ablation reduces object hallucination in open-ended generation (CHAIRi -31% relative; tuned DoLA and VCD leave it unchanged or worsen it). These results recast object hallucination in unified VQ VLMs as a property of architecture and pretraining, and yield a targeted intervention that mechanism-agnostic decoding cannot replicate.

Contributions

  • A cross-architecture L0 routing circuit present in five natural unified-VQ VLMs across three LLM families, absent in continuous-projector models, and structurally validated on a held-out model not used to calibrate the diagnostic.
  • Causal identification of the VQ bottleneck via single-variable construction with a matched-compute MLP control, and a training-time asymmetry showing the circuit is encoded in LLM backbone weights.
  • A three-gate diagnostic chain with an architecturally motivated (not threshold-fit) two-tier criterion that discriminates twenty-five models with mechanistically distinct positive and negative signatures.
  • An intervention that loses to tuned VCD and DoLA on binary calibration but uniquely reduces object hallucination in open-ended generation.

Method

We instantiate activation patching with two corruption types, Gaussian noise on visual embeddings and image-swap corruption, over a fixed set of 500 paired records (300 NaturalBench image-swap pairs and 200 POPE Gaussian-noise pairs). For each record we cache activations on the clean and corrupted runs, then re-run the corrupted input while patching in the clean activation at one (token-category, layer) site at a time. The restoration score R = (logitpatch - logitcorrupt) / (logitclean - logitcorrupt) is read out on the answer token.

Visual-embedding norms vary by more than an order of magnitude across the cohort, so a fixed noise scale would over-perturb some models and under-perturb others. For each model we sweep sigma on a logarithmic grid and take the value whose mean per-token clean-to-noisy cosine similarity is closest to 0.5. This uses only input embeddings, not predictions or labels. Sigma then sorts the cohort into three classes: projector-amplified VQ, continuous-projector, and unified-vocab VQ, where visual tokens enter the LM token-embedding table at text-token scale. Gate A2's two-tier threshold is inherited from that classification rather than fit to the cohort labels.

Gate A1 is conjunctive

The candidate circuit predicts two things: a routing event concentrated in the early layers, and persistence of the resulting signal rather than its dissipation. A1 therefore has two halves. Initiation requires the relative divergence between clean and corrupted residual streams to reach 0.4 within layers [0, 8), the first quarter of the stack. Propagation requires it to reach 0.4 again somewhere at layer 12 or later. The two ranges are disjoint and separated by a four-layer buffer, so neither can be satisfied by the decaying shoulder of the other. A model passes A1 only if both hold.

The two failure modes are populated by different models. Failing initiation means the model does not route visual information through its early layers, which is where UniTok, Show-o, SEED-LLaMA, the matched-compute MLP control and FSQ v2 land. Failing propagation means the early event is causally inert: Emu3-Chat is the one model in the cohort with a real initiation event, 0.435 at L0, that decays to a downstream maximum of 0.215.

VILA-U clears 0.4 in both ranges. The matched-compute MLP control, trained on the same data for the same 2000 steps, clears neither (0.206 initiation, 0.106 propagation).

Why the gates are applied as a chain. Individually each one admits confounds. A2 alone is passed by a model that routes visual tokens through L0 without driving a pathological signal along that route: the matched-compute MLP control has L0 mass 25.52, because a healthy Vicuna-7B already routes early visual attention there, yet it fails A1 and has a null behavioral effect. UniTok and Emu3 clear the A2 mass threshold and fail A1 from opposite directions. Qwen-VQ makes the converse point, passing A1 and A3 with a severely collapsed codebook and failing only the mass screen. Janus-Pro passes A1 because any model that grounds strongly propagates visual corruption forward, quantized or not.

Threshold sensitivity. Sweeping one gate at a time over the nineteen models carrying instrumented gate values, the partition is invariant to A3 across 60 to 95% and to A2 across 8 to 15, first changing at 18 when VILA-U, the lowest-mass positive at 17.50, drops out. A1 is bound on both sides: lowering it to 0.35 admits FSQ v2 at 0.379 and 0.30 admits UniTok at 0.347, while raising it to 0.45 drops FSQ v1. That upper bound is numerically tight, since FSQ v1's binding value is 0.449997.

Induction and the matched-compute control

Starting from LLaVA-1.6 with Vicuna-7B and CLIP frozen, we train two projectors that differ in exactly one component: one quantizes through a codebook, one does not. Same 50k CC3M subset, same 2000 steps, same hyperparameters. Only the VQ configuration installs the circuit. Panel (b) is the part that matters for the chain design: both configurations have essentially the same L0 attention mass, so the routing pathway is already in the backbone. What VQ pretraining adds is the signal that travels down it, plus the divergence that carries that signal forward.

Reversal does not work at the same budget. Training a continuous SigLIP MLP adapter on top of VILA-U's VQ-pretrained Vicuna-7B for 2000 steps leaves A1 passing (0.672 initiation, 0.647 propagation), raises L0 mass from 17.50 to 20.53, and raises the POPE yes-rate from 81% to 99.2%. It is recorded as a chain FAIL at A3 because the continuous adapter leaves no codebook to probe; the structural gates it does pass are what carry the reversal argument.

Capacity does not determine polarity. All four K values collapse to roughly 80 to 160 active codes regardless of nominal capacity, and their polarities still differ. K = 4096 is the open anomaly: a per-entry bias probe shows its codebook is strongly YES-biased at 0.836, statistically indistinguishable from K = 16384 (0.864) and K = 65536 (0.870), yet L0 ablation moves it in the opposite direction. The anomaly is in the sign of the intervention, not in the codebook.

Collapse is not the operative factor either. The FSQ v1 configuration cannot collapse by construction and installs the circuit anyway, with inverted polarity. FSQ v2 reproduces that inversion behaviorally (yes-rate +53.3 points, AMBER -19.8) but installs no gate-detectable early event: its initiation value is 0.379, and divergence first clears 0.4 only at L14. The two lattices agree on the behavioral consequence and differ on the mechanism, so the polarity inversion cannot be attributed to the early circuit alone. Together with Qwen-VQ, which collapses without installing the circuit, this leaves discretization through a codebook as the operative factor and the surviving codes' training distribution as what governs the sign.

Binary versus generative

Binary benchmarks. Tuned DoLA wins.

Open-ended captioning. Only L0 ablation falls below baseline.

We swept VCD over 16 configurations and DoLA over 20 and report the best configuration per benchmark. On POPE and AMBER, tuned DoLA reaches 0.794 and 0.795, above every mechanism-targeted intervention we tried. On a single-token forced choice a tuned decoding-time logit fix can match or beat ablation.

On CHAIR the ranking reverses. Only L0 ablation reduces CHAIRi, from 10.3% to 7.1%. This is not an artifact of shorter captions: truncating each baseline caption to its paired ablated length lowers baseline CHAIRi only to 9.53, leaving 2.40 points attributable to the intervention. The effect concentrates where hallucination actually happens. Baseline hallucination count correlates -0.383 with the change under ablation, and images with at least two baseline hallucinations lose 1.59 hallucinated objects on average while the rest are unchanged. By category, total hallucinated mentions fall from 310 to 192, with 36 categories improving, 11 worsening and 31 unchanged.

Partial doses do not transfer between the two regimes. Scaling the L0 attention output by alpha rather than zeroing it gives +8.67, +8.17 and +12.30 points on POPE at alpha = 0.25, 0.50 and 0.75, so a partial dose beats full ablation on the forced choice. Under the same sweep, only full ablation lowers CHAIRi below baseline; every partial dose raises per-object hallucination and lowers recall, shortening captions rather than grounding them.

On VILA-U itself, the model that survives the sanity criterion cleanly, L0 ablation gives +4.23 on POPE and +16.9 on AMBER; L1 ablation, targeting the downstream writer, gives +11.60 and +23.37; VTI at alpha = 0.005 gives the largest AMBER effect at +30.23. The specificity is sharp in the other direction too. The same L0 ablation is null on LLaVA-1.6 (-0.7 on POPE) and catastrophic on Qwen2.5-VL (-34.3 on POPE, -20.1 on AMBER).

The filters include the cases that do not favor the method. L0 ablation loops on 62 of the 500 images and shortens captions from 158 tokens to 78, trading recall (58.5% to 51.6%) for the CHAIRi reduction; tuned VCD loops on 130. The looping flag is our own repeated-trigram measure, not a CHAIR metric. This kind of failure is why every intervention in the paper is paired with a question-conditional sanity criterion, and why Liquid and Anole, whose L0 ablation collapses them into degenerate emitters, are used as gate-level evidence only.
